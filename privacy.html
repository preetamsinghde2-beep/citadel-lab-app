Privacy policy - citadel-lab

Last updated: 5 November 2026

  • Who uses it: only the owner of the Google account that signs in. There are no other users.
  • What it can access: only files that citadel-lab itself created in that Google Drive (Google scope drive.file). It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.
  • What it stores: the owner's own research data and analysis results, inside the owner's Drive.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. No advertising, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account under "Security" → "Third-party apps with account access".
  • Contact: the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for citadel-lab.
